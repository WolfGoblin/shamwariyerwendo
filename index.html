About us

About Shamwari YeRwendo

We walk alongside students, employees, families, the elderly and anyone who is struggling. We bring counsellors, clinicians, faith leaders and wellness practitioners together under one roof.

Our Mission

To both inspire and empower various groups of people to live healthier, support one another and flourish in every dimension of life.

Our Vision

To be one of the leading organisations creating a world where wellness is embraced as a dynamic and interconnected journey. That journey nurtures not only physical vitality but also mental clarity, emotional resilience and meaningful social connection.

What we set out to do

Our objectives

  1. 01

    Resilience workshops

    Interactive workshops on resilience, drug and substance abuse management, and coping techniques for every group.

  2. 02

    Healthy workplaces

    Promote work-life balance, and help organisations craft and implement inclusive wellness policies.

  3. 03

    Peer support in schools

    Foster positive relationships through programs that encourage peer support and mentorship in schools.

  4. 04

    Inclusive wellness

    Run wellness workshops for people living with impairments and for disadvantaged communities.

  5. 05

    Safe spaces & counselling

    Create safe spaces and offer counselling to employees, students, young people, women, men, the elderly and the disabled community.

  6. 06

    Whole-person programs

    Design programs that promote physical, financial, spiritual and mental wellbeing.

Post-rehab · Aftercare

Recovery & Aftercare Support

Leaving rehab is a beginning, not an end. For people coming home from a rehabilitation centre or another total institution, our dedicated nurse aides walk with them through the places and situations that trigger them. They help each person rebuild routines, relationships and a normal, fulfilling life.

  • One-to-one nurse aide accompaniment
  • Trigger awareness and relapse-prevention planning
  • Family support and reintegration
  • Ongoing counselling and check-ins

How it works

Starting is simple

    Reach out
